# Air Force Papers, Staff Study & Bullet Statements (Ch. 16, 17, 19)

## 1. Comparing AFH 33-337 Papers (Ch. 16, pp. 219–228)

| Paper Type | Primary Use | Headings / Flow | Format Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Point Paper** | Concise memory-jogger for fast meetings/calls (p. 222) | Flexible topic sections | Centered 3-line ALL CAPS title (`POINT PAPER / ON / <TOPIC>`), `1"` margins, single-idea dash bullets (`-`, `--`, `---`), **Page 1 ID line** bottom flush left (`Rank Last/Org/Symbol/Phone/initials/DD Mmm YY`), no signature block. |
| **Talking Paper** | Oral speaker/VIP cue card (quick facts to talk through, p. 223) | Flexible topic headings | Centered `TALKING PAPER / ON / <TOPIC>`; ultra-telegraphic phrase fragments; <=1 page ideal; Page 1 ID line at bottom. |
| **Bullet Background Paper (BBP)** | Summarize facts, options & recommendations concisely for staff/CC (pp. 224–225) | Standard 4–5: **PURPOSE**, **BACKGROUND**, **DISCUSSION**, **RECOMMENDATION** (or **CONCLUSION**) | Centered `BULLET BACKGROUND PAPER / ON / <TOPIC>`; **short narrative** sentence typical for PURPOSE & CONCLUSION/REC; **dash hierarchy** (`-`, `--`, `---`, hanging indent, single-space within, double-space between, open punctuation on fragments) for BACKGROUND & DISCUSSION. |
| **Background Paper** | Detailed narrative history & research before action (pp. 226–227) | Numbered narrative sections | Uses **narrative sentences** (not dash bullets); supports charts/tables; Page 1 ID line. |
| **Position Paper** | Persuade leadership on one advocated course of action (p. 228) | Narrative BLUF / Issue / Counterarguments / Recommendation | Follows Background Paper prose format, sharpened for persuasion and countering opposing COAs. |

- **Page 2+ Rule (All Ch. 16 Papers):** Page number `0.5"` from top flush right starting Page 2; ID line appears on **Page 1 only**.

## 2. The Staff Study Report (Ch. 17, pp. 229–236)

- **6-Step Problem-Solving Process:** 1) Analyze Audience, 2) Limit Problem (`Who/What/When/Why/How`), 3) Determine Factors, 4) Collect & Analyze Data, 5) Devise & Test COAs against criteria, 6) Select & Write Report.
- **Format:** Prepared as an **Official Memorandum** (`MEMORANDUM FOR` may remain blank until routing level is set). Five mandatory numbered headings:
  1. **`1. PROBLEM:`** Single crisp statement of what must be solved (no discussion).
  2. **`2. FACTORS BEARING ON THE PROBLEM:`** Separate labeled sub-blocks for **Facts** (verified truths), **Assumptions** (necessary unknowns held true), **Criteria** (measurable test thresholds like `"<= $6,000/yr"`), and **Definitions** (only non-obvious terms; omit unused sub-blocks).
  3. **`3. DISCUSSION:`** Pros/cons and COA evaluation tested systematically against Criteria.
  4. **`4. CONCLUSION:`** Logical outcome derived strictly from Discussion.
  5. **`5. ACTION RECOMMENDED:`** **One decisive actionable recommendation** (never *"recommend further study"* or multiple competing picks).

## 3. Writing Bullet Statements (Ch. 19, pp. 247–256)

`[MODERN DAF UPDATE]` **DAFI 36-2406 Performance Reports:** Since 2022–2023, DAF transitioned Enlisted and Officer Performance Briefs (**EPBs/OPBs** in *myEval*) from legacy compressed bullet fragments to **complete-sentence narrative performance statements** (plain language, Action + Impact aligned to Airman Leadership Qualities [ALQs], ~350-character cap). **Ch. 19 accomplishment-impact rules remain active for BBPs, Talking/Point Papers, and local staff bullet products.**

- **Accomplishment-Impact Structure (BBPs / Action Summaries):**
  - Formula: **`- Action Verb + Specific Task/Scope; Quantified Impact--Strategic/Mission Result`** (single-line goal; strong active verbs; concrete numbers rather than vague qualifiers like `"various"` or `"significantly"`).
- **BBP Bullet Mechanics vs. Action Bullets:**
  - *Single-idea bullets* (`-`, `--`, `---` hierarchy) explain facts, rationale, or sequence cleanly in a BBP.
  - *Accomplishment-impact bullets* (`Verb...; impact--result`) fit Discussion lines summarizing unit results or metrics. Both obey hanging-indent alignment and concise telegraphic phrasing.

```text
                       BULLET BACKGROUND PAPER
                                 ON
                  UNIFIED STAFF PACKAGE MODERNIZATION

PURPOSE

Summarize the automated Tongue and Quill document workflow for 1 FW/CC approval.

BACKGROUND

- Current manual Word and slide formatting causes 35 percent Front Office rework
-- Average staff package turnaround takes 9.2 duty days across three directorates
-- Multiple Department of War (DoW) and CUI variations create template drift

DISCUSSION

- Automated build scripts enforce AFH 33-337 margins, typography, and signature rules
-- Generates synchronized eSSS, Tab 1 memo, and cover e-mail from one JSON spec
-- Supports DoDI 5200.48 CUI banners and optional DoW or DoD nomenclature
- Multi-format linter flags missing attachments, undefined acronyms, and AI filler

RECOMMENDATION

Approve wing-wide adoption of the unified digital template suite effective 1 December 2026.

Maj Smith/1 FW/XP/DSN 555-0100/jts/21 Nov 26
```
