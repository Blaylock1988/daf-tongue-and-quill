# Résumé & Cover Letter (Ch. 21, pp. 265–274)

## 1. Function, Four Formats & Federal/USAJOBS Guidance (pp. 265–269)

Unlike an Official Biography (Ch. 20), a résumé is a private-sector or federal civilian job-search tool targeting a specific announcement. Keep an updated biography on hand to harvest dates, assignments, and metrics.

- **Four Formats (Form Follows Function):**
  1. **Chronological:** Reverse-chronological work history (most recent first, detailing past ~10 years); best for steady progression within the same career field.
  2. **Functional:** Groups **skills, knowledge, abilities, and achievements** by functional theme (e.g., *Logistics Management*, *Cyber Operations*) ahead of minimal job dates; best for career changers or employment gaps.
  3. **Combination:** Leads with functional capability clusters followed by reverse-chronological work/education history; **ideal for transitioning military members and retirees** translating diverse military assignments into civilian skills.
  4. **Targeted:** Tailored directly to one specific job requisition using the employer's exact key terms and competency bullets.
- **OPM / Federal Résumé Note (`[MODERN DAF UPDATE]`):** Federal applications on **USAJOBS** standardize formatting in online builders and weight explicit specialized-experience details heavily (series/grade, hours/week, salary/dates, supervisor contact). Check current OPM / USAJOBS length rules (agencies increasingly enforce concise 2-page limits on non-SES postings).

## 2. Seven Steps, Inclusion/Omission Rules & Military Translation

1. **Seven Steps to an Effective Résumé:** 1) Assess target role/skills, 2) Select format, 3) Translate military jargon into civilian business/engineering terms, 4) Quantify results (`action verb + measurable outcome`—never copy raw EPR/OPR abbreviations like `WG/CC` or `TDY`), 5) Design clean scannable layout, 6) Proofread backwards & zero typos, 7) Pair with tailored cover letter.
2. **Things to Include:** Full name, city/state, reliable phone & professional email (plus LinkedIn/portfolio if relevant), clear objective/summary, reverse-chronological achievements with metrics (`$, %, hours, headcount`), degrees/certifications, security clearance level *if required by job* (verify OPSEC).
3. **Things to Omit (Privacy / EEO / OPSEC):** Full street address (city/state suffices), SSN/DoD ID, birthdate/age, marital status, dependents, race/religion/political affiliation, photographs, height/weight, classified program names or sensitive unit operational metrics.

## 3. The Résumé Cover Letter (pp. 269–274)

- **Purpose:** Never repeat the résumé word-for-word—connect **why this organization**, **your top 2–3 relevant accomplishments**, and **request for interview** in **<=1 page** (3 short paragraphs, business-letter format, active voice).
